# CFR Society — Action as the Unit of Progress

**Layer IV.** Status: *heuristic meta-framework for human activity.*

> **Thesis:** A good life, institution, and civilization keep the admissible
> future of every agent open and high-dimensional, and avoid Roadblocks.

---

## 0. Scope and honesty

This is the most removed from Layer I. It is a **practical lens**, not
physics and not a moral proof. It must be combined with game theory and
mechanism design to handle real incentives. See §9 for guardrails.

---

## 1. Personal: freedom-volume management

- **Option value:** prefer choices that keep `N(h)` high-dimensional.
- **Personal Roadblock candidates:** addiction, debt traps, irreversible
  commitments, isolation — states that collapse future continuations.
- **Skill acquisition** literally adds dimensions to your `N(h)`.
- **Heuristic:** before a decision, ask *"does this expand or contract my
  admissible futures?"*

---

## 2. Relationships: trust and constructive interference

- **Trust** is a constraint-reducer: it makes more joint futures admissible.
- **Cooperation** is constructive interference — two agents' actions
  compounding rather than canceling.
- **Abuse / exploitation** is contraction of another's `N(h)` (the CFR
  definition of harm).

---

## 3. Institutions and governance

Judge every rule by its effect on `dim N(h)`:

- **Enabling constraints:** rights, rule of law, public goods, standards.
- **Suppressing constraints:** arbitrary power, corruption, exclusion.
- **Separation of powers** is redundancy — it raises the civilization's
  `V_F` by ensuring no single failure collapses the whole option space.

---

## 4. Justice as fair access to N(h)

- **Justice** = fair access to a high-dimensional `N(h)`.
- **Oppression** = systematic contraction of a group's `N(h)`.
- **Equity policy**, on this view, is the deliberate removal of binding
  constraints on marginalized agents' option spaces.

This gives a structural (not merely sentimental) account of injustice.

---

## 5. Civilization: existential risk as Roadblock management

Existential risks are macroscopic Roadblock candidates — states where
humanity's `N(h) → ∅`:

- unaligned artificial intelligence,
- engineered pandemics,
- ecological tipping points,
- nuclear escalation.

**Long-term governance** = engineering the global constraint structure so
that `N(h) ≠ ∅` for humanity forever. This aligns with longtermism but is
phrased in action-geometry terms: *never let the species-level option space
collapse.*

---

## 6. Constructive interference: policy alignment

Audit policy for **destructive interference** — subsystems whose outputs
cancel:

- subsidizing fossil fuels *while* mandating green transition with no bridge,
- raising rates *while* demanding growth,
- funding research *while* defunding the pipeline that supplies researchers.

**Alignment principle:** design institutions so each subsystem's output
expands the others' `N(h)`.

---

## 7. Education, science, art — freedom-volume generators

These add dimensions to humanity's `N(h)`:

- **Science** maps new regions of the possible.
- **Education** raises each person's `dim N(h)`.
- **Art** explores and communicates novel futures.

A society that defunds them is contracting its own future.

---

## 8. CFR-inspired civic indicators (speculative)

*Tag: speculative, not derived.*

- **Civic Option-Space Index:** aggregate `dim N(h)` across a population
  (mobility, education, health, legal access, economic entry).
- **Existential Roadblock Risk:** probability-weighted `P(N(h)→∅)` for
  species-level threats.
- **Policy Interference Index:** measured cancellation between major
  policy subsystems.
- **Freedom-Volume Trend:** is the society's total `V_F` rising or falling
  over time?

---

## 9. Guardrails and misuse risk

The lens can be abused. Explicit guardrails:

1. **"Freedom" is not deregulation-by-slogan.** Some constraints *enable*
   freedom (rights, safety nets); removing them can *lower* `dim N(h)`.
2. **Individual `N(h)` must be weighed against collective Roadblock risk.**
   One agent's "freedom" to emit, infect, or arm can collapse everyone's
   future.
3. **Combine with game theory.** CFR describes the option space; incentives
   determine who moves where. Neither alone is sufficient.
4. **Respect persons.** Treat agents as open option-spaces (ends), never as
   mere instruments — the CFR reading of Kant.

## 10. Relationship to the other layers

```
Layer I  physics      (speculative)   — does the algebra work?
Layer II philosophy   (interpretive)  — what does it mean to be?
Layer III economics   (heuristic)     — how is value created?
Layer IV society      (heuristic)     — how should we live?
```

Each layer borrows the previous one's vocabulary but not its proof burden.
The practical value of Layers III–IV does **not** depend on Layer I
succeeding; Layer I's success would merely give the whole edifice a physical
foundation.
